Image to Image AI FAQ

What is image to image AI?

It starts from a photo you already have and changes its look (the light, background, scene or art style) while the subject stays as you shot it.

Is image to image AI free?

New users get 3 free Standard images. Free photos are public and watermarked. After that, each photo costs credits, from 2 credits.

Does image to image AI keep my photo's subject the same?

Yes. Templates keep your product, dish, room or pet as shot. Only the light, background, scene or style changes.

How is image to image AI different from an AI image generator?

A generator invents a picture from a description. Image to image AI starts from your real photo, so what customers see is what you actually sell.

Can I use the photos commercially?

Yes. Photos made with paid credits are private, watermark-free and yours to use in listings, menus, ads and social posts.
