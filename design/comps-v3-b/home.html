Image to Image AI FAQ

What is image to image AI?

Shotviva is an image to image AI tool that gives a photo you already have a new look while the subject stays as shot — in 3 steps: upload one phone photo, pick a template, choose Standard, HD or Max. Templates cover food, products, rooms, pets and art styles; nothing to write.

Is image to image AI free?

New users get 3 free Standard images, no card needed. Free photos are public with a watermark. After that a photo costs from 2 credits: plans start at $19.90 a month for 50 credits, or buy a credit pack from $9.90.

Does image to image AI keep my photo's subject the same?

Yes. Shotviva templates keep your product, dish, room or pet as you shot it and change only the light, background, scene or art style. If something is off, open the photo, press Edit and say what to change, from 2 credits.

How is image to image AI different from an AI image generator?

An AI image generator invents a picture from a description. Image to image AI starts from your real photo, so the result shows what you actually sell.

Can I use the photos commercially?

Yes. Photos made with paid credits are private, watermark-free and yours to use in listings, menus, ads and social posts. Photos made with the 3 free Standard images are public with a watermark.

Do I need to write prompts?

No. You pick a template, and the template carries the light, scene and styling. There is one optional line, "Anything to change?", for a note like "keep the chain gold".

What sizes and formats can I export?

Three resolutions, delivered as PNG: Standard 1024 px, HD 2048 px and Max 2880 px on the long side. Eight sizes: Original, Banner 16:9, Post 4:5, Listing 1:1, Story 9:16, Rental 3:2, Website 4:3 and Pin 2:3. Uploads can be JPG, PNG or WebP up to 20 MB.

Can I use Shotviva on my phone?

Yes, in the browser on iPhone Safari or Android Chrome; there is no app to install. Take a photo with the camera or choose one from your gallery, then save or share the result from Photos.

What is Shotviva?

Shotviva is an AI photo studio for small businesses: an image to image AI that turns one phone photo of your product, dish, room or pet into a finished photo in a chosen look. You pick a template instead of describing a picture, the subject stays exactly as you shot it, and the result downloads in Standard, HD or Max as PNG. Unlike an AI image generator, it never invents your product; unlike a photographer, it takes 20–40 seconds and starts at 2 credits. New users get 3 free Standard images.
