## 中文正文 · Shotviva 推广员协议

**1. 参加计划。** 本协议由你与 Shotviva 的运营方 Grayscale Luminary LLC 订立。请用本人的 Shotviva 账号申请，如实说明推广渠道和计划，并亲自阅读、接受本协议。我们审核申请，你可以登录查看结果。仅在推广员资格有效期间，符合条件的交易才产生新佣金。使用 Shotviva 服务还需遵守网站的服务条款和可接受使用政策。

**2. 推荐归属。** 请使用自己后台提供的消费者推广链接。其中的 `ref` 标识你的推荐。访客设备上的推荐 Cookie 最长保留 60 天，以首次记录为准。系统在注册时核验推荐关系；尚未绑定、也从未付款的账号，还可在第一次付费结账时凭有效记录绑定。成功绑定后，推荐关系持续适用于该账号以后的合资格付款。归属有误时，可联系我们核查更正。个人邀请码和优惠码使用 `code`，团队招募链接使用 `by`。单独使用这两类链接均不建立消费者推广归属。

**3. 计佣。** 计划开放时，你推荐的用户购买符合条件的积分包、月付或年付会员，包括续费或升级付款，按该笔付款适用的比例计佣。基数是折后实付金额扣除税款，佣金按美元计算，向下取整至美分。具体比例取决于商品及你的个人约定，你可在推广员后台核对每笔佣金的比例和金额，或在推广前联系我们确认。比例变更只适用于后续付款。仅经单独开通团队权限的推广员，可就其直接招募的有效推广员带来的合资格消费者付款获得团队奖励；付款时双方须具备相应资格。招募、点击和注册本身均不产生佣金。计划关闭期间的付款以后不补计。

**4. 如何推广。** 请如实介绍自己的体验，并在推荐内容或链接旁用受众所用的语言清楚披露利益关系，例如「通过此链接购买，我可能获得佣金」。不得自我推荐、以多个账号制造交易、竞投 Shotviva 品牌关键词广告、在优惠券网站分发链接、发送垃圾信息、冒充官方或作出误导性承诺。我们可核查异常交易、暂停或终止违规账号的推广资格、作废违规交易涉及的未付佣金。如对处理结果有疑问，可联系我们核查。

**5. 确认、退款和争议。** 默认确认期为付款后 30 天。争议或异常交易会暂缓确认，直至处理完毕。部分退款按退款占含税实付总额的比例扣减佣金，全额退款或商户败诉时，该笔佣金归零。规则同样适用于已确认或已付的佣金；差额从余额扣回，余额为负时由未来佣金抵扣，无需另行汇款偿还这笔差额。

**6. 结算和收款。** 每月在后台显示的结算日核算一次，默认是每月 1 日（UTC），默认门槛为 200 美元。结算时须有有效推广资格、达到当期门槛并已填写收款资料。符合条件时生成结算单，由我们人工处理付款；结算日是核算日期，实际到账取决于付款处理和收款机构。余额未达门槛则结转。我们承担自己的汇出费用，你承担收款机构向你收取的费用及换汇费用。请保持收款资料准确，并按要求提供适用税务文件、承担本人应缴税款。请勿在公开渠道发送税务或收款资料。

**7. 数据、版本与联系。** 我们使用推荐标识、归属和付款记录核算佣金，推广员后台仅展示脱敏的被推荐用户信息。我们按网站隐私政策处理申请及收款资料。更新协议时，我们会在条款页公布新版本，并在需要你同意时由你亲自确认。关于申请、比例、归属、佣金或结算，请联系 support@shotviva.com。

## English text · Shotviva Affiliate Agreement

**1. Joining the program.** This agreement is between you and Grayscale Luminary LLC, the operator of Shotviva. Apply using your own Shotviva account, describe your promotional channels and plans accurately, and personally review and accept this agreement. We review applications. Sign in to check the outcome. Only qualifying transactions made while your affiliate status is active can earn new commissions. Your use of Shotviva is also subject to its Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.

**2. Referral attribution.** Use the consumer referral link in your dashboard. Its `ref` parameter identifies you. A referral cookie on the visitor’s device lasts up to 60 days and preserves the first recorded referral. We check attribution at signup. An account with no existing attribution or previous payment can also be attributed at its first paid checkout using a valid referral record. Once established, attribution continues for the lifetime of that account on qualifying payments. Contact us if an attribution needs correction. Personal invitation and discount codes use `code`. Team recruitment links use `by`. Neither establishes consumer referral attribution on its own.

**3. Earnings.** While the program is open, qualifying purchases of credit packs and monthly or annual memberships by your referred customers, including renewals and upgrade payments, earn commissions at the rate applicable to each payment. We calculate commissions in US dollars on the amount actually paid after discounts and excluding tax, rounding down to the nearest cent. Rates depend on the product and your individual arrangement. You can check the rate and amount for each commission in your dashboard or contact us to confirm your rates before promoting. Rate changes apply only to future payments. If we separately enable team participation for you, qualifying customer payments attributed to an active affiliate you recruited directly may earn you a team bonus. Both affiliates must have the applicable eligibility when the customer pays. Recruitment, clicks and signups alone earn no commission. Payments made while the program is closed do not earn commissions if it reopens.

**4. Promotion.** Describe your experience truthfully. Clearly disclose your financial relationship alongside the recommendation or link, in the language of your audience. For example, “I may earn a commission if you buy through this link.” Do not refer yourself, manufacture transactions using multiple accounts, bid on Shotviva brand keywords, distribute links on coupon sites, send spam, impersonate Shotviva or make misleading promises. We may investigate unusual transactions, suspend or terminate affiliate access for violations, and void unpaid commissions on transactions affected by a violation. Contact us to request a review.

**5. Confirmation, refunds and disputes.** The default confirmation period is 30 days after payment. Disputed or flagged transactions are held for review. A partial refund reduces the commission in proportion to the share of the total payment, including tax, that was refunded. A full refund or a dispute decided against the merchant reduces that payment’s commission to zero. These rules also apply to commissions already confirmed or paid. We deduct the difference from your balance. Future commissions offset a negative balance, without a separate repayment from you for that adjustment.

**6. Settlement and payment.** We assess balances once a month on the settlement date shown in the dashboard. The default date is the first of each month (UTC), and the default threshold is US$200. To qualify, you must have active affiliate status, meet the applicable threshold and have provided payout details. We generate a statement for eligible balances and process the payment manually. The settlement date is the calculation date. Arrival depends on payment processing and your receiving institution. Balances below the threshold carry forward. We cover our own sending fees. You cover fees your receiving institution charges you and any currency conversion fees. Keep your payout details accurate, provide applicable tax documentation when requested, and pay any taxes you owe. Keep tax and payout information out of public channels.

**7. Data, versions and contact.** We use referral identifiers, attribution and payment records to calculate commissions. Your dashboard shows only masked identifiers for referred users. We handle application and payout information under our Privacy Policy. We publish updated versions on the program terms page and ask you to confirm personally when acceptance is required. Contact support@shotviva.com about applications, rates, attribution, commissions or settlements.
